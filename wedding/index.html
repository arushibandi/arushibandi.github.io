Wedding of Arushi & Charley

January 27–29, 2028
Udaipur, India

Dear friends and family, you are warmly invited to join the wedding celebrations of Arushi Bandi and Charley Zhao.

Arushi and Charley met in October 2018 while in college at Carnegie Mellon University, where they both competed on the club tennis team together. During the pandemic, they became best friends and grew their love for each other.

When Arushi and Charley first visited the California Redwood forests in January 2025, they immediately knew it was a magical place. They returned to the Redwoods in May 2026, where surrounded by the towering trees, Charley proposed to Arushi.

For this special moment in their lives, they would be deeply blessed to have you join them at a historical palace in Udaipur, India, a city of incredible natural beauty and rich cultural heritage.

Hotel accommodations during the wedding and transport to and from the airport will be provided for our guests. Wedding activities will begin at 12PM IST on January 27th and conclude at the end of the night on January 28th. A goodbye brunch will be hosted on the morning of January 29th.
